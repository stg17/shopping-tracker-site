SHOPPING TRACKER - how to install
==================================

Shopping Tracker keeps track of your online orders by reading your order, shipping
and refund emails in Gmail: what's on the way, what to keep or return, how many
days are left to return, and whether your refund came back.

It runs only on your own Windows computer. Your emails and orders stay on your PC.

WHAT YOU NEED
  - A Windows 10 or 11 computer
  - A Gmail account (where your order emails go)
  - About $5 of Claude credit (each email costs about 1-3 cents to read)

STEP 1 - Install (1 minute)
  1. Right-click the zip file and choose "Extract All", then open the extracted folder.
     (Running Install from inside the zip without extracting it won't work.)
  2. Double-click "Install".
     If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
  3. Wait for the black window to say "Done". The app opens in your browser.

STEP 2 - Add your Claude key (3 minutes)
  1. Go to https://console.anthropic.com and sign up (you can use your Google account).
  2. Click "Billing" and add credit. $5 is enough to start.
     Tip: leave "auto-reload" off. You can never be charged more than you put in.
  3. Click "API Keys", then "Create Key", give it any name, and click Copy.
  4. Paste it into the box on the Shopping Tracker page and click "Save key".

STEP 3 - Connect Gmail (1 minute)
  A Google page opens by itself after you save the key.
  1. Choose your Gmail account.
  2. Google says it "hasn't verified this app". That's expected for this small app.
     Click "Advanced", then "Go to Shopping Tracker (unsafe)", then "Continue".
  3. Go back to the Shopping Tracker page. It starts reading your last 90 days of
     order emails, which takes a few minutes.

AFTER THAT
  - It starts by itself when you turn on your PC and checks Gmail every 30 minutes.
  - Open it any time with the "Shopping Tracker" shortcut on your desktop.
  - The app can only READ your email. It can't send, change or delete anything.
  - When your Claude credit runs out, you get a notification; add more credit and it
    picks up where it left off.

TO REMOVE IT
  Double-click "Uninstall" in this folder. It asks before deleting your saved orders.
  To remove Gmail access completely, also visit myaccount.google.com/permissions.
