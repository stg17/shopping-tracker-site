Privacy Policy

Shopping Tracker · Last updated October 9, 2026

What the app accesses

Shopping Tracker asks for read-only access to Gmail. It cannot send, delete, or change any email. It searches only for emails that look like order, shipping, delivery, return, or refund messages.

Where your data is kept

The app runs on the user's own computer. Order details it finds are stored in a file on that computer. There is no Shopping Tracker server, and no data is stored by the app's developer.

What is shared, and with whom

To read each order email, the app sends that email's text to Anthropic's Claude API, which extracts the store, items, order status, and tracking details. Before anything is sent, the app automatically tries to remove card numbers, Social Security and account numbers, phone numbers, and street addresses on the user's computer. This filtering is automatic and may not catch everything; names, email addresses, and purchase details are not removed. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models and keeps it only for a limited time, as described in Anthropic's privacy policy.

No data is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Google user data

Shopping Tracker's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to show the user their own orders.

Removing access and data

You can revoke Shopping Tracker's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's folder on your computer removes all of its stored data.
