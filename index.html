Shopping Tracker

A personal app that keeps track of online orders, deliveries, returns, and refunds.

Shopping Tracker runs on its owner's own computer. With permission, it reads order confirmation, shipping, delivery, return, and refund emails from Gmail and organizes them into a simple dashboard: what's on the way, what's been delivered, what's being returned, and whether the refund has arrived.

It is a personal project and is not offered as a public service.
